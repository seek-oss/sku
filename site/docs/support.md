# Support

sku is open source. Its primary purpose is to improve consistency, cross-team collaboration, and code quality at SEEK.

If you are not building a SEEK UI, consider alternatives such as [Vite] or [Remix].

## Getting help

sku is actively supported. Contact us early. Do not wait until an issue becomes a blocker.

That includes:

- You found a bug or unexpected behaviour
- You have a new use case that might need a sku change
- Something feels awkward, confusing, or not ideal
- You are thinking of using unsupported behaviour, or bypassing restrictions

**If you work at SEEK**, contact the [#sku-support] Slack channel.

[Vite]: https://vitejs.dev/
[Remix]: https://remix.run/
[#sku-support]: https://seekchat.slack.com/channels/sku-support
